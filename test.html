Tilt Guard

The Tilt Guard App allows you to hide your screen with ease. This app comes with two modes - Manual Mode and Auto Mode. Auto Mode uses your phone's rotation sensors to quickly hide your screen when your phone goes beyond a certain angle. You can also use manual mode to show/hide your screen.
